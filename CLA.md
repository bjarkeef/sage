## How to sign

Sign by commenting on your pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

An automated check records your signature against your GitHub account and this
agreement version. You sign once; later pull requests are recognised
automatically. If this agreement changes materially, you will be asked to sign
the new version.
